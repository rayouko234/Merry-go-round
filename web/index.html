Merry-Go-Round

Save Together, Build Your Group, Receive Your Share

Save Together

Contribute a fixed KES amount every week or month with people you trust and watch the pot grow.

Build Your Group

Create a savings group, invite members with a code, and set rules everyone can see and rely on.

Receive Your Share

A transparent rotating payout schedule shows exactly who receives the pot, cycle by cycle.

How it works

  • Create your account — name, email, phone and a password.
  • Start or join a group — set the contribution amount, frequency and member limit, or join with an invitation code.
  • Save every cycle — record your contribution; the group admin verifies it before it counts as confirmed.
  • Take your turn — payouts rotate through the agreed order, with every schedule change kept in an audit trail.
Track, don’t trust blindly. This MVP tracks contributions and payout schedules; it does not process payments. Self-reported entries are clearly marked “Pending” until a group admin verifies them, and confirmed records are never silently edited — corrections happen through audited reversals.
